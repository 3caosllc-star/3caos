    Process

    How a project runs.

    The same six stages whether we're shipping a logo, a kettle or an app. You get one point of contact the whole way through.

    01

    Discover

    Calls, research and a clear brief. We find out what success looks like.

    1–2 wks
    02

    Define

    Scope, budget and a plan, with the risky parts named up front.

    1 wk
    03

    Design

    Concepts, iterations and decisions. You see work early and often.

    2–6 wks
    04

    Engineer

    CAD, code or production files. Built to be made, not just shown.

    Varies
    05

    Build

    Prototypes, factory runs or releases. We manage vendors and QA.

    Varies
    06

    Launch

    On shelves, live online, in people's hands. Then we keep improving it.
